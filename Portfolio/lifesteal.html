Key Architectural Foundations

The **Lifesteal Plugin** brings intense hardcore survival mechanics to Minecraft. The core feature triggers a secure heart theft upon PvP kills: the victim loses a heart container while the killer claims it.

To prevent server exploitation, the plugin integrates safe health limits, custom crafting rules to synthesize hearts from rare resources, and item-based heart withdrawal ledgers. It is fully optimized for minimal CPU ticks, supporting major PvP networks.
