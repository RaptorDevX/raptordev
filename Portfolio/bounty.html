Key Architectural Foundations

The **Bounty Plugin** provides Minecraft servers with highly secure hitman and target systems. The core focuses on transaction ledger safety, blocking economy loopholes and dupes by executing atomic data locks before confirming kills.

The reward engine hooks into Vault and custom currency bridges, handling instant balance adjustments. Integrated Discord webhooks notify whole channels whenever major targets are claimed, creating dynamic PvP environments on major networks like BharatMC and IndiaMC.
