// OPERATIONS OVERVIEW

Key Architectural Foundations

The **Clan Plugin** is engineered from the ground up to solve the performance bottlenecks commonly associated with massive player-scale clan progression networks. By utilizing fully asynchronous database read/write queries and smart memory caches, main-thread event locks are kept at absolute zero, maintaining server TPS stability during heavy siege cycles.

Every transaction is safeguarded by strict state ledgers to completely block duping vulnerabilities, while permission systems give admins micro-level configurations over clan ranks, friendly fire rules, and invite approvals.
