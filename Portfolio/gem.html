Key Architectural Foundations

The **RaptorGemPlugin** completely transforms standard survival worlds by introducing late-game fantasy elements. Players invest expensive resources (like Nether Stars, Echo Shards, and Shulker Shells) to synthesize modular gems.

Designed specifically for multi-threaded region-based environments (Folia), the plugin completely avoids lag-inducing global player ticking, utilizing per-player distributed schedulers and coordinate-specific block modification locks instead.

01

Download the latest RaptorGemPlugin.jar from the releases.

02

03

Restart Folia/Paper server to warm regional scheduling tick pipes and register configs.
